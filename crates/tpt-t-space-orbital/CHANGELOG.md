## [0.1.0]

### Added
- Phase 10: 6DOF relative navigation (LiDAR, visual markers, LEO GPS),
  autonomous docking via MPC with plume impingement and keep-out management,
  7-DOF arm with damped-least-squares IK and singularity avoidance, and payload
  mass estimation.
